Scope and crew

What this mobilisation covers

Primary scope is completing every confirmed Deluge reline. Secondary scope is assessing the Hydrant configurations to decide whether they can be done on this trip or need a dedicated return.

Primary scope

Deluge relines

All eight confirmed Deluge installations, targeting two to three per day. A strong run finishes Wednesday; a conservative run finishes Thursday. Friday is held in reserve.

Secondary scope

Hydrant assessment

Assess the three known Hydrant configurations and confirm whether the equipment and material already on site can complete them.

Decision point

Demobilisation

Called immediately after the final Deluge and the Hydrant assessment. Return flights stay unlocked until the Deluge completion date is clear.

Installation methodology

WHT flange relining — sixteen steps

Worked in order at every XP location. The installation record is only marked complete once the final CCTV inspection has been accepted.

Hydrant assessment

The Hydrant works stay a separate phase from the Deluge programme. GMS internal diameter is approximately 202 mm and HDPE approximately 155 mm — a 47 mm diameter reduction, or around 23.5 mm radial transition.

Configuration 1

Straight connection

Confirm available straight installation length, access and termination positions.

Configuration 2

Bend after connection

Determine distance from the flange transition to the bend, and whether the bladder and liner can negotiate it.

Configuration 3

Sweeping bend before connection

Assess insertion direction, sweep radius, liner positioning and the ability to accurately locate the repair over the flange.

At every Hydrant

What gets assessed

Actual bore through the GMS and HDPE · distance from access point to flange · distance from flange to nearest bend · bend radius and configuration · insertion direction · available straight section either side of the flange · proposed liner length · bladder arrangement · ability to retrieve installation equipment · surface preparation access · whether additional liners, shaped bladders or other materials need to be manufactured.
